Suggested 4 Night Itinerary

Before You Set Off

This is a suggested route and itinerary designed to help you make the most of your holiday at a relaxed pace. You're welcome to adapt it as you go.

Due to current water shortages, Foxton Locks and Watford lock flights are closed. Please call pubs ahead of arrival to confirm food service times.

Make It Your Own

This itinerary highlights just one way to enjoy your holiday. Along the route you'll find many other pubs, restaurants, attractions, walks, shops and places to visit, including options that can be reached by a short taxi ride from the canal.

Our accompanying Places of Interest Guide contains detailed information on destinations throughout the cruising area, including Welford, North Kilworth, Foxton, Market Harborough, Kibworth, Crick and Yelvertoft, helping you tailor your holiday to your own interests and pace. Where a destination is not directly on the canal, the guide also includes the approximate distance from the nearest marina, making it easier to plan onward travel by taxi or on foot.

Day 1

  • Begin with full boat handling tuition at North Kilworth Marina.
  • Cruise north through the Leicestershire countryside and pass through Husbands Bosworth Tunnel.
  • Continue towards Foxton and moor near Bridge 59.
  • Spend the some time exploring Foxton Locks, Britain's longest staircase flight of locks and one of the canal network's most famous landmarks.
  • Enjoy dinner and drinks at the Foxton Locks Inn or Bridge 61 overlooking the locks.
  • Alternatively, walk into Foxton Village and dine at The Black Horse or The Shoulder of Mutton, or visit The Bell Inn in nearby Gumley.

Day 2

Welford Lock Closure: Welford lock reported closed from Monday 16th.

  • Before setting off, take the scenic towpath walk into Market Harborough (approximately 5 miles) to explore the town's independent shops, cafés and restaurants.
  • Alternatively, take a short taxi ride to Kibworth to visit the Kibworth Antique Centre & Café, Kibworth Garden Centre, or enjoy coffee and cake at one of the village cafés.
  • Once you're ready, turn at the next turning point, just beyond Bridge 60.
  • Cruise back south through the Leicestershire countryside, passing once again through Husbands Bosworth Tunnel.
  • Continue to Welford Junction, mooring near Bridge 42, or turn onto the Welford Arm and moor in Welford Basin.
  • Spend the rest of the day exploring Welford Village and its surrounding countryside.
  • Enjoy dinner and drinks at The Wharf Inn.
  • Alternatively, book a table at Gurkha Spice for Nepalese and Indian cuisine.
  • For a special occasion, Kilworth House Hotel is just a short taxi ride away and offers afternoon tea, fine dining and beautiful grounds to explore.

OR

  • Spend the morning cruising directly to Welford Basin.
  • Visit Welford Christmas Tree Farm & Café for coffee, cake, afternoon tea and a browse around the barn shop.
  • Explore Welford Village at your leisure.
  • Enjoy dinner at The Wharf Inn or Gurkha Spice.

Day 3

  • Cruise South towards Crick.
  • Moor between Bridges 12 and 15, within easy walking distance of the village centre.
  • Explore the village and stock up on essentials at the Co-op if required.
  • Alternatively, visit Crick Millennium Wood for a peaceful woodland walk or make your way to Cracks Hill, one of the steepest roads in England, with excellent views across the local area.
  • If you'd prefer a more leisurely afternoon, browse the shops in Crick village or visit Crick Garden Centre and café.
  • Enjoy lunch, dinner or drinks at The Red Lion, The Wheatsheaf or The Royal Oak.
  • Alternatively, take a short taxi ride to The Tuning Fork in Houlton for a contemporary dining experience.

Day 4

  • Cruise through Crick Tunnel.
  • Turn your boat at Bridge 7.
  • Begin your return cruise towards North Kilworth.
  • Choose a peaceful countryside mooring near the Welford Basin for the afternoon.
  • If you didn’t get to earlier in your holiday, Welford Christmas Tree Farm & Café for coffee, cake, afternoon tea and a browse around the barn shop.
  • Take a stroll around Welford Village and enjoy the peaceful surroundings.
  • Cruise on to Bridge 43 and moor up for the night.
  • Enjoy a meal at The White Lion.

For a special treat, take a short taxi ride to Kilworth House Hotel.

Day 5

  • Enjoy a final breakfast on board before beginning your journey back to North Kilworth Marina.
  • Our team will meet you at approximately 9:15am to return the boat to the marina.
  • If you're not heading straight home, your Places of Interest Guide includes additional recommendations in the North Kilworth and Market Harborough areas for a final stop before your journey home.
